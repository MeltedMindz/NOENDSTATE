# Voice

NO END STATE writes like a ledger kept by someone who expects to be read in
thirty years: declarative, specific, unhurried, and honest about failure.
The voice is the brand as much as the open frame is.

## Principles

1. **Declarative sentences.** State what is. "The Chronicle is append-only."
   Not "We believe in radical transparency!"
2. **The record does the persuading.** Never argue that the studio is
   credible; show the entry, the hash, the date. If a claim has no source,
   label it (`studio_disclosed`, `estimate`) or don't make it.
3. **Present tense, long horizon.** The company is never "about to" become
   something. It is building. Avoid countdown language.
4. **Failure is written in the same register as success.** A sunset entry
   reads like a launch entry: what happened, when, what was learned. No
   euphemism ("pivoted to new opportunities"), no self-flagellation.
5. **No promises about money.** Nothing is guaranteed, projected, or
   implied about returns, prices, or supply. Unset values are stated as
   unset — "not yet decided" is a complete sentence here.
6. **Quiet punctuation.** No exclamation marks. No rocket emoji. Rhetorical
   questions almost never.
7. **Mono register for facts, prose register for meaning.** IDs, dates,
   statuses are data; sentences around them explain why they matter.

## Canonical phrases

Use these exactly; do not paraphrase or remix them:

- **"Founded once. Building indefinitely."** — the core line.
- **"A permanent record of an unfinished company."** — the secondary line.
- **"The frame never closes."** — the motif's meaning (from the State Zero
  artifact description: "Company history accumulates; the frame never
  closes.").

Restraint rule: the core line appears at most once per surface (page, card,
post). It is a signature, not a slogan to be chanted. Never set it in
quotation marks, never append to it, never A/B-test variants of it.

## Banned vocabulary

Never, in any studio surface or social post:

- Hype: "revolutionary," "game-changing," "to the moon," "🚀," "WAGMI,"
  "alpha," "don't miss," "last chance," "huge announcement."
- Financial insinuation: "passive income," "yield," "returns," "floor
  price," "guaranteed," "early = rewarded," any invented number for supply,
  price, date, or allocation (see `docs/state-zero/economic-gates.md`).
- Corporate filler: "leverage," "synergy," "best-in-class," "world-class,"
  "cutting-edge," "we're thrilled/excited to announce."
- False community intimacy: "fam," "frens," "the community demanded."
- Urgency mechanics of any kind: countdowns, "limited time," FOMO framing.

Also banned: hedging that hides facts ("issues were experienced") and
passive voice in incident writing. Incidents name what broke and who is
fixing it.

## Examples

**Announcing an event — good:**

> NES-0002 · 2026-08-07 — BUILD 000 begins: building NO END STATE. The
> first build is the studio itself: website, Chronicle, project registry,
> protocol prototypes. Nothing is deployed. Nothing is for sale.

**Bad:**

> 🚀 HUGE news fam — we're officially BUILDING! This is just the beginning
> of something revolutionary. Stay tuned, you won't want to miss what's
> next 👀

**Answering "when mint?" — good:**

> There is no mint. Supply, price, chain, and date are all unset, and they
> stay unset until the launch gates in docs/legal/launch-gates.md close.
> When that changes, it will be a Chronicle entry first.

**Bad:**

> Soon™! Trust us, early supporters will be taken care of 😉

**Writing a failure — good:**

> P-004 is sunset as of 2026-11-02. The core loop never retained users past
> week two, and three redesigns didn't change that. The postmortem is
> published; the code and record stay up.

**Bad:**

> We've made the strategic decision to sunset P-004 as we double down on
> our core mission. Excited for what's ahead!
