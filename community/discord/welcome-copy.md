violation. Report scams in #scam-reports; appeal moderation via
#contact-the-team or the email on noendstate.com.

## #how-no-end-state-works

**How NO END STATE works**

**The studio.** NO END STATE is an onchain product and protocol studio. It
was founded once — August 7, 2026 — and is designed to build indefinitely.
No exit, no final form. The company itself describes it as a permanent record
of an unfinished company.

**The Chronicle.** Everything material that happens gets a numbered entry in
the Chronicle, the append-only company record at noendstate.com/chronicle.
Entries have stable IDs (NES-0000 and counting). Corrections are appended as
new entries; nothing is rewritten or deleted. The #milestones, #releases,
#incidents, and #postmortems channels here mirror it.

**The projects.** The project registry is currently empty. That is not a
placeholder problem — it is the honest state. No project has launched yet.
The first build, BUILD-000, is the studio itself: the site, the record, the
tooling, this server. When projects launch, each gets a registry entry
(P-000, P-001, ...) and its own category here.

**STATE ZERO.** STATE ZERO is the founding cohort — a fixed NFT collection
that will mark the people who were here at the beginning. Current status,
stated plainly: **minting is not active, no contract is deployed, and no
supply, price, or chain has been published.** There will never be a second
founding collection. STATE ZERO is not an investment, and holding it will
entitle you to being early — nothing more is promised. When any of that
changes, it changes on noendstate.com and in #official-links first.

**The lab and the graveyard.** Experiments run in the open in THE LAB.
Failures are recorded in #failed-experiments and #graveyard, not deleted.
The record includes what didn't work — that's most of what a record is.

**What we ask of you.** Read the rules, report scams, and hold us to the
record.
