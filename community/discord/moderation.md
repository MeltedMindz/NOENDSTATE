# Discord moderation

Policy and playbook for keeping the server usable. Moderation exists to
protect conversation and members, not to protect the studio from criticism.
Critical feedback stays up.

## Rules

The member-facing short version lives in `community/discord/welcome-copy.md`
(#rules copy). The operational reading:

1. **No scams, no phishing, no impersonation.** Fake mints, fake links, fake
   team accounts, wallet-drainer links, "support" DMs. Zero tolerance —
   this skips the ladder and goes straight to ban plus report.
2. **No harassment or hate.** Attacks on people are out; attacks on ideas are
   fine.
3. **No financial advice, no price talk as a service.** STATE ZERO is not an
   investment and nothing here is financial advice. Speculation-as-content
   (price predictions, "when mint" spam) gets removed.
4. **No unsolicited promotion.** No shilling tokens, servers, or products.
   #show-your-work exists for sharing real work.
5. **No spam.** Includes mass mentions, copy-paste across channels, engagement
   farming, and off-topic flooding.
6. **Stay in channel topic.** Soft rule; moderators redirect before removing.
7. **English is the working language** for moderated channels, so moderation
   can actually read what it moderates.

## Escalation ladder

Applied per member, tracked in the private moderation log channel. Severity
can skip steps; scams always skip to step 5.

1. **Redirect** — in-channel nudge, no record beyond the message itself.
2. **Note** — logged observation, no member-facing action. Builds pattern.
3. **Warning** — explicit, logged, delivered by reply in-channel (not DM,
   consistent with the no-team-DM policy; the member can be pointed to
   #contact-the-team to discuss).
4. **Timeout** — Discord timeout, 1 hour to 7 days depending on pattern.
   Logged with reason.
5. **Kick or ban** — kick for a pattern that timeouts did not fix; ban for
   scams, impersonation, harassment campaigns, and ban evasion. Bans are
   applied by Security, Operator, or Founder (Moderator escalates; see
   `community/discord/permissions.md`).

## Moderator playbook

- **Delete and log.** When removing content, capture a screenshot or message
  link in the moderation log before deletion where practical, with a one-line
  reason. The log is append-only in spirit: entries are not edited later.
- **Scam content:** delete immediately, ban immediately, post the pattern to
  Security for a possible #security-alerts notice, and thank the reporter in
  #scam-reports if it was reported. Speed over ceremony.
- **Heated but honest argument:** do not delete. Slow it down: thread it,
  redirect it, or ask both sides to pause. Delete only rule breaks.
- **Criticism of NO END STATE:** not a moderation matter. Ever.
- **Gray areas:** prefer the lighter action, ask in the moderator channel,
  and write down what was decided so the next call is consistent.
- **Never moderate in DMs.** All moderation is visible in-channel or in the
  logged actions. Moderators never DM members first, same as everyone on the
  team.
- **Conflicts of interest:** do not moderate a dispute you are part of;
  hand it to another moderator or Operator.

## Appeals

A banned or sanctioned member can appeal by email to the address listed on
noendstate.com, or via #contact-the-team for anything short of a ban.
Appeals are reviewed by Operator or Founder — not by the moderator who acted.
Outcomes are logged. Scam bans are not appealable in practice, but appeals
are still read.

## Transparency

- Moderation statistics (actions by type, not names) may be published to the
  Chronicle periodically. Individual cases are not publicized.
- If moderation itself fails badly — a wrongful ban, a missed scam wave —
  that is incident material and follows the postmortem process like any
  other failure. The record includes our mistakes; that is the point of the
  company.
