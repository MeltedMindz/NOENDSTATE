# X content pillars

What the account posts about, and how often. The account is the public
building record — it narrates real work. If nothing happened, nothing is
posted; silence is more honest than filler, and the record's credibility is
the whole asset.

## Pillars

### 1. What we're building
Current work, narrated plainly. BUILD-000 today (the studio itself); real
projects when the registry has them. Screenshots of actual interfaces,
links to actual commits.

### 2. Why it exists
The thesis. No-exit design, permanence, the economics of not ending.
Manifesto material, cut into posts. This pillar carries the identity;
overuse makes it preachy — a few times a month, not daily.

### 3. Build logs
Entries from noendstate.com/build, posted as they land. What got built,
what got decided, what got cut. The bread and butter of the account.

### 4. Project launches
When a project ships: the launch thread from
`community/x/project-launch-template.md`. Rare by definition — the registry
is empty today, and launches are announced when they are real, never teased.

### 5. Technical mechanisms
How things work under the hood: contract design, protocol mechanics,
architecture decisions, with links to public code. Written for technically
literate readers; no dumbing down, no jargon-flexing.

### 6. Chronicle events
Material events posted as they are recorded, from
`community/x/chronicle-template.md`, always linking the numbered entry.
The account's spine: everything important eventually appears here.

### 7. Failures and postmortems
Failed experiments, incidents, postmortems — posted with the same energy as
wins, from `community/x/postmortem-template.md`. This pillar is what makes
the record believable. Never buried on a Friday night; posted when ready.

### 8. Treasury transparency
Treasury-relevant events, mirroring noendstate.com/treasury. Dormant until
there is something to report; when there is, it is reported plainly and on
schedule, not selectively.

### 9. STATE ZERO history
The founding cohort as record, not as promotion. What STATE ZERO is, its
current honest status, and — when terms are ever published — each fact as
it becomes real. Never scarcity marketing, never countdowns, never "soon."

### 10. Builder recruitment
Open invitations to contribute: the public repo, RFCs, bounties when they
exist, Contributor paths. Aimed at people who make things, written like a
colleague talking, not a hiring funnel.

## Cadence guidance

- **Steady state:** roughly 3-5 posts a week, dominated by pillars 3, 5,
  and 6 — the work itself. One pillar-2 (why) post most weeks at most.
- **Quiet weeks:** post less. Never manufacture activity; an invented
  metric or fake milestone would poison the record permanently.
- **Launch weeks:** the launch thread plus follow-up answers; suspend the
  regular cadence rather than stacking it.
- **Incidents:** security and incident posts (see templates) preempt
  everything and follow their own timing — fast first notice, thorough
  follow-up.
- **Threads vs posts:** single posts for events and log entries; threads
  for mechanisms, launches, and postmortems. No thread longer than it
  needs to be.
- **Engagement rules:** answer honest questions, ignore bait, never DM
  first, never quote-dunk. No engagement farming: no "gm", no polls-as-
  content, no reply-guy behavior from the studio account.
